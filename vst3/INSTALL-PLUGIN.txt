AFTERGLOW 0.3.0 — NATIVE DELAY TEST

1. Close Ableton before replacing the plugin.
2. Extract Afterglow-Windows-VST3 from the successful GitHub Actions run.
3. Replace the entire Afterglow.vst3 folder in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
4. Open Live 11, Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan, then load Afterglow.

STARTUP: Delay OFF, EQ OFF, Wow/Flutter 0%, Master Mix 100%, Output gain 0 dB.

- Turn Delay ON; try Sync, 1/8, Dotted, Feedback 35–45%.
- Wow adds slow tape drift; Flutter adds faster movement. Try one at a time at 20–40%.
- EQ ON enables the Low Cut (high-pass) and High Cut (low-pass) sliders.
  EQ shapes the complete processed delay signal, including the first echo and its core signal.
- Master Mix 0% is the original; 100% is the complete processed signal including EQ.
- Double-click any numeric readout, type a value, and press Enter. Escape cancels.
  For frequency, examples are 2500, 2500 Hz or 2.5kHz. Cutoffs each cover 20 Hz–20 kHz.
- Feedback Tone/Wear are removed. Their old automation will no longer apply.

Use a short phrase with space afterward for listening. Echoes can increase peak level; leave headroom.
The remaining modules and the final artwork are not ported yet. Report sound and control behavior in chat.
